Welcome to the Agentic AI Vantage study blueprint

This study track is a practical quick-reference and visual architecture companion for the Google Cloud Professional Agentic Architect certification, using Rosku Bank (a fictional enterprise bank) enterprise case studies to illustrate design trade-offs. Because Google Cloud’s agentic AI platform evolves rapidly, this course is continuously refined—always refer to the official Google Cloud documentation for exhaustive product specifications.

2. How to use this study companion

1. Review the domain roadmaps

Start each section with its Domain roadmap (1.0 through 5.0) to review the core architectural themes, download the offline PDF, and access the official Google Cloud exam guide reference.

Step through interactive SVG flow simulators, 1-line concept definitions, and decision matrices across all five sections to see how enterprise guardrails, runtimes, and protocols behave under the hood.

3. Practice scenario simulators

Test your architectural judgment with anti-length-bias practice questions featuring instant rationales, architecture strips, and topic filters.
